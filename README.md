Student Assistant app built with Base44. Still in demo mode.
Student Assistant is your all-in-one helper with studying, managing your grades, feeling creative or just memorizing things better.
With Student Assistant, you can do a lot of useful things:
• view and manage your grades (E-Dnevnik) by filtering subjects or semesters you attended,
• view Statistics about your total studying time, average grades or average time spent studying per day,
• take Notes during class or practice time to memorize important parts of the lecture,
• use Tools to edit/upload your documents, photos or other types of files of your homework,
• chat with friends or study mates in the in-built private Collaboration space,
• express your creative art on the canvas in Self-Ideas,
• you can manage Notifications and Appearance in the Settings,
• and lastly, a built-in AI companion ready to help your needs of studying and anything else...

Log in or create an account with Google's Gmail (currently supported).
